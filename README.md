Royal Rebound
A cursed knight. A bouncing ball. One way back.

The King cursed you, his loyal knight, and turned you into a bouncing ball. Bounce through three rooms of his castle, find the key in each one, and defeat the King to break the curse and bounce back

Made for the Hack Club Haven Bangalore Game Jam.

How to play
In every room you come in through the entrance door. Head to the door at the far end to make the key appear, then grab the key on your way back and unlock the gate above the entrance to reach the next room. After the last room, you face the King.

Action	Keys
Move	A / D or Left / Right
Big bounce	S, Down or Up, tapped just before you land
Shoot	Hold left click (shoots where your mouse points)
Restart the room	R
How to play

Features
Three rooms, each with its own look and layout: The Dungeon, The Gardens and The Frozen Throne
A boss fight against the King, who charges, slams the floor to send out spike waves, and summons bats
Enemies and hazards: slimes, rats, bats and a mimic, plus moving platforms, spikes and water
Story scenes with a typewriter effect, and a comic style how to play screen
Music, sound effects and saved settings for music volume, sound volume and fullscreen
Run it yourself
Install Godot 4.7.
Open Godot, click Import, and choose this folder's project.godot.
Press F5 to play.
