# Royal Rebound

A 2D pixel art platformer where you play as a ball that never stops bouncing. Get through three castle rooms, grab the keys, and beat the King in a boss fight

Made for the **Hack Club Haven Bangalore Game Jam**. 

## Controls

- **A / D** or **Left / Right** to move
- **S**, **Down** or **Up** just before landing for a big bounce
- **Hold left click** to shoot
- **R** to restart the room

## Run it

Play it in your browser on [itch.io](https://nivashraj.itch.io/royal-rebound), or open `project.godot` in [Godot 4.7](https://godotengine.org/) and press **F5**
